THE PART NOBODY TELLS YOU

THE WAVE LASTS 90 SECONDS.
YOU DO THE REST.

The chemical wave of an emotion rises, crests and clears in about ninety seconds. What keeps it alive for six hours is you replaying the story that started it.

RISE · 0–30s

NAME IT, DON'T NARRATE IT

"This is anger." Two words. Naming an emotion lowers amygdala activity; telling its story turns the volume back up.

CREST · 30–60s

RIDE, DON'T DECIDE

Nothing important gets decided at the crest. Send no message, sign nothing, quit nothing. Breathe out longer than in.

FALL · 60–90s

DON'T RE-RUN THE TAPE

The wave will fall on its own. Every replay restarts the chemistry — this is the whole mechanism.

REPLAY · AFTER

SURF, THEN CHOOSE

An urge is a wave too, usually cresting inside 10–30 minutes. You do not have to obey a wave.
